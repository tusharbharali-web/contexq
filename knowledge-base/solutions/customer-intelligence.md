## Every customer, understood from every angle.

Once the connections are clear, ContexQ reads each customer for risk and opportunity, so you know where to act first. The six customer scoring domains (around "Your customer", on a Risk ↔ Opportunity scale):

1. Financial health: Spot stress early.
2. People and ownership: Know who decides, and who already knows them.
3. Footprint: See where the business really operates.
4. Money movement: Read intent in how money flows.
5. Sustainability: Back real progress, not greenwashing.
6. News and events: Know what matters before it reaches your book.

## How ContexQ helps you win the right clients.

ContexQ does the digging across records, registries and local news, so you know who to call, why it matters and who can introduce you. Four scenarios (the page states "Scenarios are illustrative."):

1. Find clients you didn't know existed: Links the people and companies behind your customers. A quiet account's director, for example, turns out to run several growing companies (Company A, Company B, Company C) that don't bank with you yet ("Not yet your clients"). Outcome: New clients to approach.
2. Walk in through a warm door: Shows who already knows whom, so you start with an introduction, not a cold call. A shared agent (registered agent) reveals a family group (business group), and the founder's daughter already banks with you. Outcome: A warm way in.
3. Hear it before everyone else: Reads local-language news and notices before they reach English media. When your borrower's directors join a competitor, you hear about it first. Outcome: A head start on the news.
4. Be first with the right financing: Connects your customers' suppliers to contracts and tenders as they appear. A Mandarin tender (tender notice, "采购公告") leads to a supplier's parent winning a major contract, and you offer the financing first. Outcome: First with the financing.

## Become the bank they can't replace.

When you understand a customer's whole network, you bring more than products, and that is hard for anyone else to copy.

1. You bring business, not just products: Introduce customers to buyers, suppliers and funding schemes they hadn't found.
2. You price the whole picture: Bank both sides of a supply chain and price the group together, so staying costs less than starting again elsewhere.
3. You're already trusted: Their decision-maker knows you, and your understanding of their business deepens every year. A new bank starts from nothing.
