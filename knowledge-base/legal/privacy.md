# ContexQ Privacy Policy

The ContexQ Privacy Policy (Page: /privacy-policy), last updated November 2026, explains how ContexQ Pte Ltd processes personal data of people using the site https://contexq.com/ and its services. It names the data controller and its address, the personal data collected (directly, from employers, and automatically through cookies), the legal bases and purposes for processing, sharing with service providers, how long data is kept (logs are kept for one (1) year), and visitors' rights, including complaining to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC). Questions go to inquiry@contexq.com.

Last updated: November 2026.

## General Information

This Privacy Policy covers the processing of your personal data when using the site https://contexq.com/ (the "Site") by "ContexQ", as well as the services offered therein (the "Services"). "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. Please carefully read this Privacy Policy before using or accessing the Site.

If you would like to obtain information about how the joint controllership operates and its essential arrangements, please contact ContexQ at the details above.

## Personal data we collect

ContexQ will collect personal data about you from a variety of sources, including information collected from you directly (e.g. when you register and use the Site); from third parties (e.g. from your employer) or automatically (e.g. through cookies).

Personal data ContexQ may collect directly from you:
- Identification data (e.g. name, surname, email address, and telephone number);
- Login information (e.g. user name and password);
- Internet profiles (e.g. IP address, web browser, use of the Site and device information);
- Economic and financial data (e.g. users' selected portfolio and preferences);
- Employment data (e.g. employer and position);
- Behavioural information (data collected using cookies and other device identifying technologies); and other information provided at your discretion from time to time.

Personal data ContexQ may collect from third parties: ContexQ may obtain from your employer certain information, such as your professional contact details (e.g. name, surname, email address, and telephone number).

Personal data ContexQ may collect automatically: when using the Site, ContexQ automatically collects certain information from you which may include personal data. Further information about the use of cookies and tracking technologies is available in the Cookie Policy (/cookie-policy).

## Enquiries, callback and meeting requests

When you send ContexQ an enquiry through the contact form or the website assistant, or ask to be called back or to arrange a meeting, ContexQ receives the details you enter (such as name, email address, phone number, company, country and message) and, for requests made in the website assistant, the recent messages of that conversation. To help the team respond, ContexQ's server adds: your approximate location (city and country) and the name of the network you are connecting from, both derived from your IP address using the hosting provider and IPinfo; the domain of your email address; and, only if you have accepted analytics cookies, how you first arrived at the site, the pages you viewed and your Google Analytics identifier. This information is sent to the team by email (and may be recorded in an internal spreadsheet) and is used only to handle your request and follow up with you. It is kept for as long as needed to manage the enquiry and any resulting relationship.

## Analytics and company identification

With your consent, ContexQ uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used, and Leadfeeder to recognise which organisations visit the site based on the company network a visit comes from. These tools are not used until you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw consent at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. See the Cookie Policy (/cookie-policy) for details.

## How we use your personal data and on what legal basis

ContexQ uses and processes personal data on the following legal bases and for these purposes:

1. To manage and control your registration and use of the Site, on the basis of the execution of the Legal Notice.
2. On the basis of ContexQ's legitimate interest, for the following purposes:
   - To manage the contractual relationship entered into with your employer, by which you have access to the Site;
   - To manage the Site, including the protection of the security and/or integrity of the Site and ContexQ's IT infrastructure;
   - To deal with your enquiries and requests;
   - To understand how you use the Services and to improve and further develop the features, performance and support available on the Site, which may entail the provision of anonymous statistical information about visitors (however, without it being used to identify any individual user);
   - To contact you with promotional e-mails relating to products and services offered by ContexQ, unless you have expressly manifested your desire to opt out of such communications, or ContexQ is otherwise legally prevented from doing so; and
   - For internal administrative purposes with regard to ContexQ.

   Where ContexQ processes personal data in fulfillment of its own legitimate interests, it shall always balance such interests against the data subjects' fundamental rights and freedoms, and implement robust safeguards to ensure that their privacy is protected accordingly. You may obtain information on the balancing test upon request.
3. To comply with Singapore laws, which may include the disclosure of your personal data and other complementary information to comply with requests received from Singapore authorities and/or bodies with compelling power.

## How do we share your personal data and with whom?

ContexQ will be able to share your personal data with service providers. These service providers support ContexQ's IT infrastructure and other services and may need to access and process your data on ContexQ's behalf, to the extent necessary to provide the service. For more information on the appropriate safeguards in place, contact ContexQ at the details above.

## For how long do we store your personal data?

ContexQ will keep your personal data for as long as it has a relationship with you and insofar as the agreement with your employer is in force. Afterwards, ContexQ will retain your personal data for a period of time that enables it to defend itself against potential legal and contractual actions (during their expiration period). Finally, once these actions expire, ContexQ will delete your information. Notwithstanding the above, logs will be retained for one (1) year for security purposes.

## What rights do you have over your personal data?

You have certain rights regarding your personal data, including the right:
- To know how ContexQ is processing your personal data and to access your personal data held by ContexQ;
- To request the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
- To request the erasure of your personal data when, among other circumstances, such data is no longer necessary for the initial purposes for which it was initially collected, in accordance with applicable law;
- To restrict ContexQ's processing of your personal data, under certain circumstances (in which case ContexQ will only retain the personal data for the exercise and/or defence of ContexQ's rights);
- To object to ContexQ's processing of your personal data, having regard to the given circumstances and for reasons related to your particular situation (in which case ContexQ will only retain the personal data for imperative legitimate reasons or the exercise and/or defence of ContexQ's rights);
- To request the portability of your personal data, which will allow you to obtain and reuse the personal data in a usable electronic format for your own purposes and across different services without hindrance to usability, including its transmission to another third party;
- To withdraw the consent you may have granted to a specific processing activity, at any time; and
- To make a complaint to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of Singapore.

To exercise any of these rights, send a letter or an e-mail using any of the postal or email addresses included in Section 2 (the Data Controller details above: 23 Keppel Bay View, 25-73, 098414 Singapore; inquiry@contexq.com).
