# ContexQ Cookie Policy

The ContexQ Cookie Policy (Page: /cookie-policy), last updated November 2026, explains how ContexQ uses cookies and similar devices on the site https://contexq.com/ to enhance visitors' online experience and to carry out statistical analysis of traffic. It defines cookies, lists the types of cookies, names the cookies used (the consent cookie cc_cookie, Cloudflare Turnstile, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, the cq_attr local storage item, and Leadfeeder), and explains how to accept, reject or change cookie choices, including the cookie banner and the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. Questions go to inquiry@contexq.com.

Last updated: November 2026.

## Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how ContexQ uses cookies and similar devices (altogether, "Cookies") to enhance the online experience of the visitors to the Site https://contexq.com/ (the "Site") and to carry out statistical analysis of the traffic on it. If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, contact ContexQ at inquiry@contexq.com.

## What are cookies?

ContexQ uses the term "cookies" to refer to all technologies that store and access information on the device or browser that you use to access ContexQ's Site, such as your computer, tablet or mobile phone. For example, ContexQ uses HTTP cookies, which are small data files (typically made up of numbers and letters) that are downloaded when you access ContexQ's Site and allow ContexQ to recognise your device. They are useful pieces of technology that help improve the functions of the Site and its content by providing different types of information.

See the Privacy Policy (https://contexq.com/privacy-policy/) for more information about the use of cookies that entails processing of personal data.

ContexQ uses cookies to enhance the online experience of visitors and to better understand how ContexQ's Site is used. Cookies may tell ContexQ whether you have visited the Site before, or whether you are a new visitor.

## What types of cookies exist?

There are many different types of cookies. The main categories are:

1. According to the ownership of the cookie:
   - First-party cookies: those sent to the user's computer or device from ContexQ's computer, device or domain.
   - Third-party cookies: those sent to the user's computer or device from a computer, device or domain that is not managed by ContexQ, but by a third party.
2. According to the lifespan of the cookie:
   - Session cookies: those designed to collect and store information while the user visits a website.
   - Persistent cookies: those in which the information stored in the computer or device may be accessed and processed over a longer period of time (which can vary from a few minutes to several years).
3. According to the purpose of the cookie:
   - Analytical cookies: those used to carry out measurements and statistical analysis of the Site's usage.
   - Advertising cookies: those that allow for the management, in the most effective way possible, of the advertising spaces included on the Site (should there be any).
   - Behavioral advertising cookies: those that allow for the management, in the most effective way possible, of the advertising spaces that the publisher has included on a website, application or platform from which the requested page is provided.
   - Technical cookies: cookies required for website navigation or security.
   - Personalization cookies: those that allow the user to access the Site using predefined generic features based on a series of criteria set by the user's computer or device (such as, among others, the language).

## Which cookies do we use and for what purposes?

When you first visit the site, ContexQ asks for your choice in a cookie banner with the buttons "Accept all", "Reject all" and "Manage preferences". Only necessary cookies are used until you choose. Analytics and marketing cookies are set only if you accept them, and you can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. Your choice is kept for six months.

The banner has three categories:
- Necessary (always on): required for the site to work.
- Analytics: Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and the cq_attr local storage item.
- Marketing: Leadfeeder.

The Cookie Policy table lists:
- cc_cookie: Technical (necessary). Owner ContexQ (first party). Duration: 6 months. Stores the cookie choices you made in the cookie banner. Always active; you can change the choices it stores in Cookie settings.
- Cloudflare Turnstile: Technical (necessary). Owner Cloudflare, Inc. (third party). Duration: Session. Protects the contact form from spam and automated abuse. More information: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/. Required to send the contact form.
- Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_*): Analytic. Owner Google LLC (third party). Duration: up to 2 years. Measures how the site is used: pages visited, how visitors arrive (for example from search or a campaign link), approximate location and device type. Set only if you accept analytics cookies. More information: https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207. Change in Cookie settings, or with the Google opt-out at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en.
- Microsoft Clarity (_clck, _clsk): Analytic. Owner Microsoft Corporation (third party). Duration: up to 1 year. Produces heatmaps and session recordings that show how pages are used. Set only if you accept analytics cookies. More information: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/clarity/setup-and-installation/cookie-list. Change in Cookie settings.
- cq_attr (local storage): Analytic. Owner ContexQ (first party). Duration: until cleared. Remembers how you first arrived at the site and the pages you viewed; if you send an enquiry, this is included so the team understands your interest. Stored only if you accept analytics cookies. Change in Cookie settings.
- Leadfeeder (_lfa): Marketing. Owner Leadfeeder / Dealfront (third party). Duration: up to 2 years. Recognises which organisations visit the site, based on the company network a visit comes from; it does not identify individual people. Set only if you accept marketing cookies. More information: https://www.leadfeeder.com/privacy/. Change in Cookie settings.

## How can you exercise your choices regarding the installation/removal of cookies?

You have the right to choose whether or not to accept cookies. You can configure your browser to accept all or part of the cookies, reject/block all or part of the cookies, withdraw the consent already granted to all or part of them, or to notify you when a cookie is set. If you reject all cookies, you may not be able to use ContexQ's Site and take full advantage of all the Services. However, not all Services require that you accept cookies.

If you don't want ContexQ's Site to use analytics or marketing cookies, choose "Reject all" in the cookie banner, or open "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page to change your choice at any time. You can also follow the instructions in the table above. The policy lists these browsers for setting cookie preferences: Firefox, Chrome, Internet Explorer, Safari, Opera.

Most mobile devices provide control over tracking for advertising purposes via the "Limit Ad Tracking" setting (iOS devices) or the "Opt out of Interest-Based Ads" setting (Android devices).

Your choice to refuse cookies is specific to the browser that you are using. If you opt out on one device and want the opt-out to apply to a different device as well, you have to opt out from that device too.

For further information about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set on your device and how to manage and delete them, visit http://www.allaboutcookies.org.
