## Questions and answers

### How does ContexQ add value if we already have AML monitoring?

Existing rules and models can identify suspicious activity, but may lack context around linked parties or accounts. ContexQ adds relationship intelligence that can help teams investigate networks rather than isolated alerts.

### How is connected AML analysis different from transaction-only monitoring?

A transaction-only view focuses on the characteristics of a payment. Connected analysis also considers who is involved, how accounts relate, shared identifiers and the paths through which funds may move.

### Can ContexQ help identify hidden money-mule networks?

Yes. ContexQ links accounts, phones, devices, sign-up details and payment paths to reveal clusters that appear unrelated when reviewed one at a time.

### Can ContexQ support AML investigations and case preparation?

It is designed to bring together relationships, relevant signals and evidence paths that investigators can inspect. This can support triage and documentation, while the bank remains responsible for decisions and regulatory reporting.

### Does ContexQ automatically determine that a customer is laundering money?

No. A network connection or alert is not proof of wrongdoing. ContexQ supplies context and prioritised findings for authorised investigators to assess using the institution’s procedures.
