## Questions and answers

### How does ContexQ help an e-commerce business?

ContexQ brings together customer, order, payment, promotion and marketplace information to support connected customer understanding and investigations into coordinated abuse.

### Can ContexQ help identify promo or loyalty abuse?

It can help connect accounts that share relevant identifiers, such as devices, addresses, payment instruments or behavioural patterns. This gives teams a broader basis for reviewing suspected coordinated misuse.

### What kinds of refund and return fraud can benefit from connected analysis?

Examples include repeated or coordinated returns, suspicious refund destinations and networks of apparently separate accounts. The value comes from understanding the shared relationships across events.

### How can the same data support customer growth?

A connected view can reveal when apparently separate profiles belong to a relationship or household, where lawful and relevant. That context can inform service, retention or cross-sell decisions.

### Does ContexQ only serve large online marketplaces?

No. ContexQ supports marketplaces, direct-to-consumer brands, omnichannel retailers and other digital commerce models. Suitability depends on the data, decision and business case.
