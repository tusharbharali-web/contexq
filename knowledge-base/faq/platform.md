## Questions and answers

### What does ContexQ do?

ContexQ helps organisations make better use of data they already hold. It connects records about people, companies, accounts, suppliers and events, enriches them with relevant external information, and reveals relationships that may change a business or risk decision.

### What is contextual decision intelligence?

It is the use of relevant relationships, events and evidence around an entity to inform a decision. Instead of judging one account, supplier or customer in isolation, teams can consider the wider network and the signals affecting it.

### How does ContexQ work?

The approach is to resolve records that refer to the same entity, connect relationships between entities, add relevant external signals and present evidence-backed findings to decision-makers. The same connected foundation can support several business workflows.

### Why not just use our existing reports and dashboards?

Dashboards are valuable for the information they have been designed to show. ContexQ adds the links between data points, such as common owners, shared devices or upstream supplier dependencies, that can be hard to see in separate reports.

### What makes ContexQ different from a traditional analytics tool?

ContexQ centres analysis on resolved entities and their relationships, not only on tables or isolated events. This can make the same information useful across investigation, monitoring and growth use cases, while letting teams review the evidence behind a finding.

### Does ContexQ replace our existing technology?

ContexQ works as a connection and intelligence layer alongside existing CRM, ERP, banking, analytics and case-management tools. The objective is to enrich current workflows, not require every system to be replaced.

### Which teams can benefit from the same connected foundation?

Depending on the organisation, compliance, risk, procurement, fraud, operations, finance, sales and customer teams can use different views of shared entities and relationships. Each team should see the context relevant to its own decisions.
