## Questions and answers

### What is ContexQ?

ContexQ is a company building contextual intelligence capabilities to help organisations connect data, relationships and external signals for better decisions across risk, operations and growth.

### Which industries does ContexQ focus on?

ContexQ serves financial services, manufacturing, e-commerce, insurance, telecommunications and the public sector, with solutions spanning supply chain, KYC, AML, fraud, customer intelligence and risk.

### How can I discuss a ContexQ use case?

Start with the business question you need to answer, the systems involved and the decision you want to improve. Contact the team through the contact page or email inquiry@contexq.com to discuss a suitable next step.

### Where can I find current roles at ContexQ?

Visit the Careers page for the latest openings, or write to careers@contexq.com.
