## Questions and answers

### What business problems can ContexQ address for telecom operators?

ContexQ covers subscriber and household intelligence, cross-sell and retention, enterprise relationships, SIM-related fraud, international revenue-share fraud and dealer or supplier risk.

### How is telecom customer intelligence different from a billing-system view?

Billing platforms usually see subscriptions and charges. Connected intelligence can relate mobile, broadband, household and enterprise records to help teams understand the wider customer relationship.

### Can ContexQ help detect SIM-swap or synthetic subscriber activity?

Where the appropriate logs are available, connecting SIM, device, onboarding, customer and dealer records can reveal suspicious shared identifiers or coordinated activation patterns for investigation.

### What is international revenue-share fraud (IRSF), and how can context help?

IRSF can involve traffic driven to premium-rate international destinations. Connected analysis can help teams examine relationships among SIM activations, dealers, routes, usage patterns and destinations.

### Can telecom operators use ContexQ for dealer risk?

Dealer activations, commissions, customer records and external company information can be connected to help investigate unusual clusters or potentially related parties. Suspicious patterns should be validated by authorised teams.
