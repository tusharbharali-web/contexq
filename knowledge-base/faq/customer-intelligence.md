## Questions and answers

### We already have a CRM. What does ContexQ add?

A CRM records interactions and known customer details. ContexQ is designed to connect identities, related businesses, households and external context, helping teams see opportunities and risks that are not obvious in one profile.

### How is Customer Intelligence different from a single customer view?

A single view combines records about one customer. Connected customer intelligence also considers relationships around that customer, such as group companies, households or linked business entities, where appropriate data is available.

### Can ContexQ identify cross-sell or relationship-growth opportunities?

ContexQ can find related companies and household connections that may be relevant to growth. Teams still decide whether an opportunity is suitable and how to engage.

### Can ContexQ support customer segmentation and personalisation?

Resolved profiles and contextual relationships can provide better inputs for segmentation and campaign planning. Actual targeting must follow data-permission rules and the organisation’s own marketing policies.

### Will our analytics team still be able to use its own models?

The intention is to provide better connected data and signals to existing analysts and workflows. Teams can retain their preferred modelling and decision processes, subject to the agreed integration.
