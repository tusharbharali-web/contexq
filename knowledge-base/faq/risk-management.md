## Questions and answers

### How does ContexQ support counterparty risk assessment?

It can connect a counterparty to its owners, related companies, key customers and suppliers, alongside relevant external signals. This helps risk teams assess exposures that may sit beyond the entity’s own financial statements.

### What are early warning signals?

They are changes or events that may justify closer attention, such as adverse news, ownership changes, financial stress, trade restrictions or disruption affecting a connected party. Signals support judgement; they are not a guarantee of future outcomes.

### Can ContexQ identify indirect portfolio concentration?

Connected analysis can show when several counterparties depend on a common company, geography, sector or supplier. Risk teams can then investigate whether these shared exposures change the portfolio picture.

### Does ContexQ replace credit scoring models?

It is designed to complement existing assessments with relationship context and external information. A bank can decide how, and whether, those additional inputs are used in its approved models.

### Can risk teams trace why a particular entity was flagged?

The platform emphasises explainable findings, including the connections and source information behind a signal. This gives reviewers a basis for validating or rejecting a concern.
