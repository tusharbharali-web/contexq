## Questions and answers

### What manufacturing problems does ContexQ focus on?

ContexQ addresses hidden supplier dependencies, material and part sourcing, production continuity, supplier ownership changes, quality traceability and evidence for trade or responsible-sourcing reviews.

### Can a risk at one upstream supplier affect several production lines?

Yes. Different Tier 1 suppliers can converge on a common sub-tier facility or material. Mapping those relationships can help reveal concentration hidden behind a seemingly diversified supplier list.

### How can ContexQ help procurement and engineering work together?

Starting from parts, bills of materials and supplier records allows teams to discuss risk in terms of components and product lines, not only company names. Engineering still validates technical alternatives and specifications.

### Can quality teams trace a defect through the supply network?

ContexQ links products and materials back to suppliers, which can help investigators find possible common causes across products or plants where records support the trace.

### Can finance teams understand the business impact of a disruption?

Connecting supplier dependencies to affected parts, plants or orders can help inform an impact assessment. Exact revenue, cost and recovery estimates require the manufacturer’s own operational and financial assumptions.
