## Questions and answers

### How can ContexQ support insurance investigations?

Connecting claims, policyholders and associated parties can help special-investigation teams identify shared providers, witnesses, addresses or other relationships across claims that initially look unrelated.

### Can it help uncover organised claims fraud?

Yes. Claims across different lines of business may be connected through a common claimant, professional or service provider. Investigators still verify the evidence before taking action.

### How can connected customer data help insurers grow?

A wider picture of policyholder and household relationships can help identify protection gaps or relevant product opportunities, subject to data-use permissions and suitability checks.

### Does it work alongside policy and claims systems?

Yes. Policy administration, claims platforms, underwriting and CRM are relevant internal sources. A scoped integration can bring connected context into existing workflows.

### Can ContexQ assist underwriting risk assessment?

Ownership, related parties, location and other contextual data may inform how a risk is evaluated. Underwriting standards, approved models and final coverage decisions remain with the insurer.
