## Questions and answers

### What can network intelligence reveal that individual fraud alerts miss?

Separate accounts may share a phone, device, recruiter, payment destination or unusual timing. Linking these clues can help investigators see a coordinated ring rather than a queue of unrelated alerts.

### Can ContexQ help detect newly opened mule accounts?

Yes. New accounts can be checked against known patterns and connected entities, so relevant relationships can be flagged before an account has a lengthy transaction history.

### How can ContexQ reduce duplicate investigation effort?

Related alerts can be reviewed as one connected case with the parties and evidence mapped together, rather than asking several investigators to reconstruct the same network independently.

### Does ContexQ replace our fraud detection engine?

No. ContexQ works alongside existing fraud engines, rules, models and case managers, with connected context improving the information available to each control.

### Who makes the final decision to block or review a transaction?

The bank’s authorised team. ContexQ provides recommendations and evidence for human review, rather than independently taking enforcement action.
