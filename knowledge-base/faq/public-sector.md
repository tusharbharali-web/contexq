## Questions and answers

### What public-sector use cases does ContexQ support?

Use cases include tax and revenue intelligence, customs, procurement integrity, welfare and subsidy fraud, anti-corruption, law enforcement and financial-intelligence investigations.

### How can graph intelligence help identify procurement conflicts?

It can help reveal connections between bidders, owners, officials and awarded contracts, using available and authorised records. Reviewers then assess whether a connection creates a reportable conflict.

### Can ContexQ help detect duplicate identities or benefit claims?

Entity resolution can assist with identifying records that may belong to the same person or connected group. Any eligibility or enforcement decision must follow the agency’s legal and procedural safeguards.

### How can customs teams use connected intelligence?

Links among importers, exporters, ownership, shipments, counterparties and relevant lists can support review of complex trade relationships. Such findings remain leads for authorised customs assessment.

### Does connecting government data remove privacy obligations?

No. Access, purpose, data sharing and retention must remain governed by applicable law and agency policy. Cross-agency connectivity should only be implemented where permissions and safeguards allow it.
