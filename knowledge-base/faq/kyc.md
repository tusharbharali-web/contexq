## Questions and answers

### How does ContexQ improve an existing KYC process?

It can connect customer records with related entities, ownership relationships and external signals, giving reviewers more context than an isolated customer file. The aim is better-informed reviews within the institution’s existing KYC controls.

### Can ContexQ help uncover indirect beneficial ownership?

Graph-based relationship analysis can help trace ownership chains and links between companies and people, subject to available registry and client data. Analysts can then assess the evidence against the relevant ownership and control rules.

### Can KYC reviews become more event-driven?

Connecting monitored changes to the customer network can help flag profiles that may need review, such as ownership changes or relevant adverse events. Review schedules, thresholds and decisions remain governed by the institution’s policies.

### Does ContexQ replace our KYC or CLM system?

It is positioned as an additional intelligence layer. Existing onboarding, customer lifecycle management, screening and approval workflows can remain in place while teams use richer connected context.

### How are possible sanctions or adverse-media connections handled?

A connected relationship or possible name match is a lead for assessment, not a confirmed finding. The reviewer should be able to trace its source, assess its relevance and decide the appropriate next step.
