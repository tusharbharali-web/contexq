## Questions and answers

### Where can ContexQ help a bank?

ContexQ focuses on four banking priorities: understanding fraud and financial-crime networks, improving customer due diligence, seeing risk around borrowers and finding valuable connected customer relationships.

### How can one foundation support both risk and growth?

The same resolved company or customer relationships can inform multiple questions. A relationship that reveals indirect exposure in one workflow may also identify a group-company opportunity for a relationship manager.

### Can ContexQ fit into our existing banking controls?

ContexQ is an intelligence layer alongside AML, fraud, KYC and credit systems. It can inform their workflows with relationship context while existing governance and approvals remain in place.

### Does ContexQ make lending or sanctions decisions automatically?

No. ContexQ does not independently block payments, rate borrowers or contact clients. People review the evidence and decide under their institution’s policies.

### How is an indirect sanctions link treated?

As a potential match requiring verification. An ownership path, a similar name or a connected party may justify a review, but it does not establish a sanctions violation without further assessment.
