## Your suppliers look safe. The weak spot is behind them.

Your purchasing system only shows who you buy from. When many of your suppliers quietly rely on the same source, one problem can stop production and delay customer orders.

## How ContexQ helps from what you buy to what you ship.

Better choices before you make, steadier production while you make, and clear answers when you're asked. The section has three stages, each with two scenarios. Each scenario shows where it happens on the path Part or material → Product → Production line → Customer order, and which teams are involved.

### Before production — Choose parts, materials and suppliers you can depend on.
- **Choosing a new part or material**: Pick inputs whose supply will stay dependable once they become part of your product. Happens at: Part or material. Teams: Engineering, Procurement.
- **Adding a new supplier**: Know who owns them and whether anyone behind them is a problem, before they join your product. Happens at: Part or material. Teams: Procurement, Compliance.

### During production — Keep production steady.
- **Scaling up a product line**: Plan the scale-up knowing what each line leans on, so growth doesn't stall on a hidden weak spot. Happens at: Production line. Teams: Plant operations, Procurement.
- **When a supplier changes hands**: Hear about new owners or new links in time to respond. Happens at: Part or material. Teams: Procurement, Risk.

### When you're asked — Answer customers and regulators with confidence.
- **A customer or regulator asks where materials come from**: Give a clear, sourced answer, the same way every time, across plants. Happens at: Customer order. Teams: Compliance, Sustainability.
- **A quality problem comes back**: Follow it back to the supplier or material behind it, across products, and fix the cause. Happens at: Product. Teams: Quality.

## Be ready when someone asks for proof.

Keep clear, sourced evidence on suppliers and owners for the rules that apply to you. Your compliance team makes the final call.

Five areas on the manufacturing page, each with the rules and lists it names:
- **Trade controls and sanctions**: Check suppliers and their owners against sanctions and denied-party lists. OFAC sanctions; EU sanctions; UK sanctions; US Entity List; ITAR and EAR.
- **Human rights and due diligence**: Show where materials come from and who handled them along the way. UFLPA; EU CSDDD; EU Forced Labour Regulation; German Supply Chain Act.
- **Responsible sourcing**: Trace minerals and materials back to the smelters and mines behind them. Conflict minerals (3TG); EU Conflict Minerals Regulation; EU CBAM; EU Deforestation Regulation.
- **Anti-bribery**: See who really owns and controls the companies you pay. US FCPA; UK Bribery Act; Beneficial ownership checks.
- **Quality and standards**: Trace a defect back to the plant and supplier it came from. ISO 9001; IATF 16949; AS9100.
