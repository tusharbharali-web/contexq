# Backing & Affiliations

The ContexQ Backing & Affiliations page (Page: /Ecosystem/affiliations) lists the venture and innovation programmes that back ContexQ, the industry bodies ContexQ is a member of, and the technology companies' startup programmes ContexQ is part of. It has three groups: Ventures & Innovation Hubs (3 entries), Industry Memberships (5 entries) and Technology Programmes (6 entries). ## Ventures & Innovation Hubs

Section description: "Venture and innovation programmes that back ContexQ's growth."

### ADB Ventures

ADB Ventures (Asian Development Bank) "Serves as the dedicated venture investment arm of the Asian Development Bank, financing high-impact enterprise technologies to foster sustainable development across Asia-Pacific."

### The LHoFT

The LHoFT (Luxembourg House of Financial Technology) "Serves as Luxembourg’s national fintech innovation hub, backing ContexQ through the Catapult: Inclusion SE Asia program alongside ADB Ventures and the Luxembourg Ministry of Foreign and European Affairs."

### HKSTP

HKSTP (Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation) is "Hong Kong's flagship statutory incubator and research platform, providing deep-tech enterprises with infrastructure, global funding networks, and access to the Greater Bay Area."

## Industry Memberships

Section description: "Industry bodies and networks ContexQ belongs to."

### Singapore FinTech Association (SFA)

"Singapore’s primary cross-industry fintech body, crucial for policy advocacy, cross-border fintech corridors, and collaboration across the financial ecosystem."

### Singapore International Chamber of Commerce (SICC)

"One of Asia’s longest-standing business chambers, vital for establishing corporate governance, advocacy, and direct connections with multinational business leaders."

### SGTech

"Singapore’s leading technology trade association representing the digital economy, helping technology companies scale, access industry insights, and drive digital transformation."

### Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTP)

"Connects enterprises to a prestigious cross-border scientific research alliance and regional tech network to foster cross-disciplinary innovation and academic collaboration." (HKSTP appears both under Ventures & Innovation Hubs and under Industry Memberships.)

### UNESCO IRCAI AI4SDG Programme

"An international research initiative operating under the auspices of UNESCO that guides the deployment of ethical, trustworthy AI solutions aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals."
