# Awards, Grants & Recognition

The listing shows 17 cards in six groups: Funding & Grants (4), Global Rankings (4), Winners (2), Finalists (3), Programmes (1) and Recognition (3). - Category: Funding & Grants; badge: Funded; place and year: Singapore · 2026
- Awarding body: Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)
- Domain: Anti-Money Laundering & Graph AI

ContexQ was awarded a 312K USD grant supported by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) under the Financial Sector Technology and Innovation (FSTI) scheme to deploy advanced Graph AI and Entity Resolution against complex money mule networks and synthetic corporate identities across borders.

**Institutional Context & Problem Space:** Traditional rule-based monitoring tools evaluate transactions in isolation, generating massive false-positive volumes while failing to detect organized mule syndicates that disperse illicit funds across layered bank accounts, shell companies, and cross-border digital payment corridors.

**Technological Innovation Evaluated:** ContexQ leverages proprietary graph analytics and entity resolution algorithms to construct real-time transaction networks across disparate banking datasets, identifying circular flows, common beneficial ownership, and coordinated mule behaviors that remain invisible to legacy flat-file monitoring systems.

**Systemic Impact & Deployment Roadmap:** The grant accelerates the deployment of ContexQ's platform across financial institutions, providing compliance officers with contextual, explainable network visualizations that turn fragmented data points into clear, prosecutable intelligence for regulatory reporting and asset recovery.

**Key Milestones & Evaluation Benchmarks:**
- Awarded 312K USD Proof of Concept grant support under the MAS FSTI scheme.
- Engineered explainable multi-hop graph algorithms tailored to MAS regulatory and AML compliance expectations.
- Validated scalable entity resolution models capable of unifying ambiguous customer records across multiple institutional datasets.
- Established structured deployment pathways with commercial financial institutions in Singapore and Southeast Asia.

Quote: "Sovereign backing from MAS under the FSTI scheme reinforces ContexQ's core thesis: combating modern systemic financial crime requires moving beyond single-transaction alerts to contextual, graph-based structural intelligence." (Ajay Kakarania, Founder & CEO, ContexQ)
