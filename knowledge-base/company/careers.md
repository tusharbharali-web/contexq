## Early enough to shape the product. Proven enough to know it works.

ContexQ started in March 2024. Since then, its platform has earned top places at the G20 TechSprint, the Reserve Bank of India's HaRBInger challenge and the World AI Conference. A group small enough to fit around one table made that happen.

Being small means your work reaches customers fast. You'll sit close to the founders, hear directly from the institutions ContexQ serves and see the effect of what you build within weeks rather than quarters.

ContexQ's leaders have spent decades at Google, AWS, Oracle, Maybank and Quantexa. They share what they know openly and expect to learn from you in return.

Three highlights:
- Recognised early: Top places at the G20 TechSprint, the Reserve Bank of India's HaRBInger challenge and the World AI Conference.
- Experienced leadership: ContexQ's leaders bring decades of experience from Google, AWS, Oracle, Maybank and Quantexa.
- Based in Singapore: ContexQ is headquartered in Singapore, and that is where the team works.

## A few habits we hold each other to.

You'll notice them in code reviews, customer calls and the small choices made every day.
- Show your evidence: We ask how you know, not who said it. Good reasoning settles debates.
- Own the outcome: Take a problem from first question to finished result, with support whenever you ask.
- Write it down: Clear notes let a small team move as one.
- Respect the stakes: Our customers fight fraud and financial crime, so care and accuracy come before speed.

## Four steps, and you'll always know where you stand. (Hiring process)

Every application gets a reply. If ContexQ decides not to move forward, it will tell you so plainly.
1. Intro call: A 30-minute chat about your experience and what you want from your next role.
2. Skills session: A practical conversation built around real problems we've solved. No trick questions.
3. Meet the founders: Hear where the company is heading and ask anything you like about life here.
4. Offer: A written offer with full details, plus the time you need to decide.

## Support that lets you do your best work. (Benefits)

Exact terms depend on your country and role. Your offer letter spells out everything.
- Flexible hours: Plan your day around focused work. We measure results, not time online.
- Learning budget: Courses, certifications and conferences that grow your skills are covered.
- Health cover: Medical insurance for you, with options to include your family.
- A share in the upside: Early team members can take part in our employee equity plan.
