## Hero: Most organisations see a fragment. We show the whole network.

ContexQ is a Singapore-headquartered decision intelligence company. Our platform, SarasW, resolves fragmented data into real people, companies and relationships across names, scripts and borders, so banks, corporates and governments can see the whole network behind every decision.

Link: "Our story" (scrolls down to the story section).

## Important decisions depend on connections no single source can show.

Who really owns a company. Who a customer is connected to. Which supplier depends on which. The answers decide whether a bank takes on a client, a corporate signs a contract or an agency opens an investigation.

Yet the information sits in separate places: different internal systems, company registries, ownership records, sanctions lists and news, written across more than a dozen scripts and languages. Each source shows a fragment. The connections between them, where risk hides and opportunity sits, stay out of view.

Company founding: ContexQ's founder, Ajay Kakarania, saw this first-hand across more than twenty years in banking, risk and financial crime, including leading solutions for Asian banks at Quantexa. ContexQ was incorporated in Singapore in March 2024 to close that gap with native cross-script entity resolution and composite graph AI, with clear evidence behind every connection.

Founder quote, Ajay Kakarania, Founder and CEO, ContexQ:
"When institutional counterparties operate within opaque networks, risk is never absent; it is simply deferred. Our objective is to replace assumption with clear, verifiable context."

## One intelligence layer. Reused across the decisions that matter.

SarasW joins an organisation's own records with trusted external sources and resolves them into one connected view, reusable across risk, compliance, revenue and investigation teams. SarasW works in three steps:

1. Resolve: Records are matched to the real people and companies behind them, across names, aliases, identifiers and more than a dozen scripts, with provenance preserved.
2. Infer: Ownership chains, counterparties, directors and behaviour are brought together on a graph, so hidden links, concentrations and changes over time become visible.
3. Explain: Every insight arrives as a role-aware decision pack, with evidence, confidence, the relationship path and a suggested next step. People make the call.

Applied today across: financial crime and AML; mule and payment fraud; perpetual KYC and KYB; credit and SME intelligence; and supply chain and ESG.

## Clearer context helps organisations, and the economies they serve.

When connections are visible, decisions become faster, fairer and easier to explain.

For organisations:
- Risks surfaced earlier, before they become losses
- Fewer false positives; analyst time spent on real exposure
- Hidden revenue found in relationships you already hold
- Decisions that stand up to boards, auditors and regulators

For society:
- Financial crime, fraud and mule networks harder to hide
- Supply chains more resilient to geopolitical disruption
- Greater transparency in who owns and controls what
- AI that explains itself, with evidence over black-box scores

## How we build matters as much as what we build.

Four principles guide every product decision ContexQ makes:
- Context over volume: Knowing how people and companies connect is worth more than more rows of data.
- Clear reasons, always: Every insight can be traced, checked and challenged by your specialists.
- People decide: We rank, explain and recommend. Your teams make the call.
- Your data stays yours: Kept within your boundary and never used to train outside models.
