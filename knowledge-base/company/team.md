# ContexQ Team: Founders and Advisors

The Team page (Page: /team) shows "The founders and advisors behind ContexQ." ContexQ has three founders (Ajay Kakarania, Founder and CEO; Jean-Michel Coeur, Co-Founder and CTO; Meenakshy Iyer, Co-Founder and CPO) and five advisors (Joo Seng Wong, Prof. Dr. Simon See, Ritesh Jain, Partha Dasgupta and Gilbert Kohnke). The same team appears in the homepage coin's Team slice (on phones the Team slice opens /team) and in the team section of the About page (/about), which uses slightly different wording; both versions are recorded below.

## Team overview

- /team page heading: "Team" with the line "The founders and advisors behind ContexQ." The page has two groups, FOUNDERS and ADVISORS. Each card shows the name, position and a one-line credential, a LinkedIn link and a "See profile" button that opens the full profile (bio, Previously at / Affiliations, View on LinkedIn).

Groups: "Founders & leadership" and "Advisors". Each card has a short summary and "Read profile", which opens a profile with a tagline and description.

## Founders

### Ajay Kakarania - Founder, CEO

- Position: Founder, CEO (About page: Founder & CEO).
- Credentials: CFA · FRM · INSEAD Global EMBA.
- Previously at: Quantexa, Teradata, Standard Chartered.
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/kakarania/
- Bio on /team: Ajay began as a coder and grew through analytics lead to pre-sales across five banks (Standard Chartered, UOB, Credit Suisse, Deutsche Bank, Barclays) and three product companies (Quantexa, Avaloq, Teradata). He holds the CFA and FRM, a master's in Mathematical Trading & Finance (London) and an INSEAD Global Executive MBA. At ContexQ he leads the vision of combining graph intelligence, AI and domain expertise.
- About page tagline: "Building enterprise decision intelligence".
- About page summary: 20+ years across banking, risk and financial crime; previously led solutions for Asian banks at Quantexa. FRM, CFA, INSEAD Executive MBA.
- About page profile: Ajay brings over 20 years of experience across banking, financial services and enterprise technology in India, the UK and Singapore. His career spans risk management, regulatory analytics, financial crime, trade finance and enterprise AI, most recently leading solutions for Asian banks at Quantexa. He is a certified Financial Risk Manager (FRM, GARP), Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) and Sustainability and Climate Risk professional, and holds an INSEAD Executive MBA and a Master's in Quantitative Finance. At ContexQ, he leads the company's vision of combining graph intelligence, AI and domain expertise to help enterprises make faster, context-aware decisions.
- Also on the About page: Ajay Kakarania founded ContexQ, which was incorporated in Singapore in March 2024. His quote: "When institutional counterparties operate within opaque networks, risk is never absent; it is simply deferred. Our objective is to replace assumption with clear, verifiable context."
