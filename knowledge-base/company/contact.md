## See ContexQ in action (walkthrough request form)

"Request a personalised walkthrough built around your business challenges."

Form fields:
- First name (required)
- Last name (required)
- Business email (required)
- Company (required)
- Country (required): a drop-down list of countries, plus "Other". Choosing "Other" shows a required field "Please specify your country".
- What would you like to explore? Options:
  - Fraud & Money Mule Detection
  - Financial Crime Compliance
  - Supply Chain Intelligence
  - Enterprise Risk Management
  - Other (shows a required field "Please specify what you'd like to explore")
- Message (optional): "Tell us about your requirements".

Submitting:
- The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. Visitors must complete the security check first; otherwise the page shows "Please complete the security check before submitting."
- Note under the button: "By submitting, you agree that ContexQ may contact you about your enquiry. Protected by Cloudflare Turnstile."
- After a successful submission, the form is replaced by a confirmation: "Thank you. Your request has been sent. Our team will contact you at [your email] shortly.", with a reference number. If sending fails, the page shows an error and suggests emailing inquiry@contexq.com. The page does not state a response time.
