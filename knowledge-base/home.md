# ContexQ - Context in Every Decision (Homepage)

The ContexQ homepage (Page: /) introduces ContexQ and its Graph Intelligence Platform, SarasW. It explains that ContexQ connects entities, signals and relationships across an organisation's systems into one resolved view, lists the six Solutions and six Industries with links, shows how ContexQ works (Connect, Understand, Act) and how it fits alongside existing systems, and ends with a "Talk to the team" call to action. This file also describes the site navigation (header menus, homepage coin/pie hub and footer) so visitors can be pointed to the right page.

- Site description: "ContexQ resolves every entity once and connects them continuously, graph AI for fraud detection, financial crime compliance, and enterprise risk analytics."

## One connected view across your organisation.

Sub-heading: "The same person, scattered across systems. Brought together into one picture."

Customer information is often split across KYC, documents, transactions, and contact systems, each holding only part of the story. ContexQ connects these sources and brings them together into a single, resolved view of each customer, so your teams work from the full picture.

The homepage shows a before-and-after diagram, labelled "Illustrative example" (a fictional person, not a real customer):

Before - "Fragmented across systems" (Unlinked). The same person appears under different names in five systems:
- KYC system (Pending check): Name "J. Smith" - Verification queue.
- Documents (On file): Name "John Smith" - Passport, proof of address.
- Core banking (Active ledger): Name "Smith, John A." - Account & counterparties.
- CRM (Client profile): Name "John Smith" - Contact & relationship notes.
- Screening (Watchlist scan): Name "J. A. Smith" - Flag review pending.

After - "Connected into one resolved view" (Single Picture). The sources KYC, Docs, Banking, CRM and Screening flow through ContexQ into one resolved entity:
- John A. Smith - Resolved entity. Master entity ID: #CTX-8902-JAS.
- Identity: Verified individual (from KYC).
- Documents: Passport verified (from Docs).
- Accounts: Primary checking & linked (from Banking).
- Contact: Unified phone & address (from CRM).
- Risk flags: No active sanctions (from Screening).

## Why connections get missed.

"The risks and opportunities that matter most are often several steps away." Three challenge cards:
- System Teams: Data lives in silos, so no one sees the full picture.
- Hidden Proximity: Risk hides beyond your direct relationships.
- Investigation Burden: Finding alternatives or tracing links takes manual effort.

## Solutions built on one connected foundation.

"Intelligence for the decisions that matter." Six solution cards, each with a "Learn more" link:
- Supply Chain Intelligence (/solutions/supply-chain-intelligence): See beyond direct suppliers and identify alternatives when you need them.
- KYC (/solutions/kyc): Build a clearer understanding of who you are doing business with.
- Fraud (/solutions/fraud): Spot suspicious patterns by connecting related activity.
- Customer Intelligence (/solutions/customer-intelligence): Bring together a connected view of each customer relationship.
- Risk Management (/solutions/risk): Understand where exposure builds up across your network.
- Financial Crime Intelligence (AML) (/solutions/aml): Trace hidden links to support investigations and compliance.

## Built for organisations where connections matter.

"Wherever relationships drive risk or opportunity, context makes the difference." Industry cards:
- Financial Services (/Industries/finance), the featured card: Connect counterparties, suppliers, and customers into one risk picture.
- Manufacturing (/Industries/mfg): Understand supplier networks and dependencies.
- E-commerce (/Industries/ecommerce): Connect customers, sellers, and activity to reduce abuse.
- Insurance (/Industries/insurance): Link policies, claims, and entities for better assessment.
- Telecom (/Industries/telco): Make sense of complex customer and network relationships.
- Public Sector (/Industries/public-sector): Bring clarity to complex, interconnected data.

## Clearer visibility. Better decisions.

"Designed to help teams work with context, not guesswork." ContexQ works in three steps, each with a matching benefit:
- Step 1 - Connect: Link scattered internal and external records into cohesive entity graphs. Benefit: See hidden relationships - Find connections that were previously hard to spot.
- Step 2 - Understand: Surface indirect exposures, clusters of behaviour and multi-tier dependencies. Benefit: Investigate with full context - Work from connected information in one place.
- Step 3 - Act: Support compliance reviews and risk mitigation with clear, traceable context. Benefit: Decide with confidence - Make better-informed decisions with fuller context.

Closing line of the section: "Less manual effort spent piecing data together."

## Works alongside what you already have.

Sub-heading: "Add context to your existing systems."

ContexQ is designed to complement your current tools rather than replace them, bringing together information you already hold and adding the connections between it.

The architecture flow on the homepage:
1. Your Existing Systems: Core Banking / ERP; CRM & Client Records; External Watchlists & Registries.
2. ContexQ Engine: Entity Resolution & Graph Correlation.
3. Insights: Actionable connected view for operational clarity.
